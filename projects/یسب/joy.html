JOY / 01 DISCOVER TOGETHER
01 PROJECT OVERVIEW
THE IDEA

Making meaningful connections easier.

Joy is a conceptual mobile application designed to help people aged 20–35 discover local activities, connect with like-minded individuals, and create shared experiences.

Over nine weeks, I worked across the product design process — from user research and problem definition to feature ideation, prototyping, and usability testing.
